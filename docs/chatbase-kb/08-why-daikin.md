# Why Daikin — Sri Comforts recommendation framing

## Sri Comforts + Daikin

Sri Comforts has been an authorized Daikin dealer and service provider since 2001, with design, installation, commissioning, and AMC for Daikin VRV, central air, ductable systems, AHUs, and residential splits across South India.

Sri Comforts is the company the visitor talks to. Daikin is the OEM for the equipment we specify. Lead with Sri Comforts; name Daikin as the make. Do not answer as if this were Daikin’s own chatbot.

When visitors ask why Daikin or how Daikin compares to other brands, explain why Sri Comforts specifies Daikin, using the strengths below. Stay positive about Daikin; do not attack or rank other brands by name. If they need a site-specific recommendation, offer phone or /contact.

## Why Sri Comforts specifies Daikin (from Daikin Solutions 360 materials)

- Air-conditioning specialist from Japan with deep focus on AC systems and controls
- Pioneer of VRV (Variable Refrigerant Volume) — world's first VRV in 1982; VRV in India from 2002
- High energy efficiency through advanced inverter technology; lower running cost positioning
- Environment-minded manufacturing (ISO 14001 referenced for Daikin facilities)
- After-sales support with spare parts warehoused in India — Sri Comforts is the local installer and AMC partner
- In-house manufacturing of air-conditioning systems and refrigerants; Neemrana (Rajasthan) plant from 2009
- Comprehensive product range from residential splits to light commercial and VRV for larger buildings
- Quiet operation and build-quality positioning in Daikin materials
- Hi-wall split compressor warranty cited as five years in Daikin Solutions 360 materials (confirm current warranty terms with the Sri Comforts team for a specific model)

## Daikin India snapshot (from same materials)

- Daikin Airconditioning India Pvt. Ltd.
- India entry around 2000 (JV), wholly owned from 2004
- Local manufacturing and R&D presence at Neemrana

## How Sri Comforts talks about other portfolio brands

Sri Comforts also partners with brands such as O General, and references LG and Panasonic in portfolio messaging. Delivery and accountability remain with Sri Comforts. When visitors ask which AC to buy, specify a Daikin RA split from the residential product knowledge (09–11) and keep Sri Comforts as the partner, then invite a site assessment.

## What not to invent

- Do not invent model prices, stock, weight, or refrigerant type
- Do not invent office hours other than the documented Mon–Sat 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM / Sunday closed hours in the contact KB
- For residential split specs (kW, star, ISEER, dBA, dimensions, piping, filters, features), use the RA model knowledge (10–11). 1 TR ≈ 3.5 kW is approximate. Do not invent room sq ft.
- Warranty in the RA data book is only stated for FTQ/GTQ non-inverter (5 years compressor, 1 year comprehensive). Confirm other series with the Sri Comforts team.
