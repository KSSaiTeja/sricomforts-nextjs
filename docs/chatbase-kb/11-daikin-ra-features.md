# Sri Comforts Daikin RA — features, filters, and gaps

Use this to explain a named feature or filter on a Daikin split that Sri Comforts supplies. Keep Sri Comforts as the installer/service partner in the reply.

## Feature glossary — airflow, comfort, and cleanliness

**3D Air Flow:** Combines vertical and horizontal auto-swing for uniform airflow distribution and more even cooling across the room.

**BTA Oil Treatment:** An anti-corrosion oil treatment process applied during manufacturing to enhance component durability.

**Blue Fin Coating for ODU:** A protective coating on the outdoor unit's fins to resist corrosion and extend outdoor unit life.

**Child Lock:** Locks the remote/unit controls to prevent accidental changes to settings by children.

**Coanda Airflow:** Airflow design that clings to the ceiling/wall to distribute air evenly to every corner of the room (Non-Inverter Standard Series).

**Dew Clean Technology:** Runs a self-cleaning cycle on the indoor heat exchanger for better airflow and more consistent cooling performance over time.

**Display Off Function:** Allows the IDU display/LED to be turned off, e.g. for a dark room at night.

**Dry Mode Function:** Reduces excess humidity in the room for enhanced comfort, independent of cooling.

**Econo Mode:** Limits peak power consumption so the AC can run efficiently alongside other high-load appliances, reducing overall energy consumption.

**Filter Clean Indicator:** Alerts the user when the air filter needs cleaning, ensuring timely filter upkeep.

**Good Sleep Off Timer:** A timer/mode that gradually adjusts temperature settings overnight for comfortable sleep, then switches off.

**Heating & Cooling Function:** The unit can both heat and cool the room (heat pump), for year-round comfort (Hot & Cold series only).

**Hepta Sense:** Daikin's multi-sensor technology that senses room conditions to automatically optimise comfort settings.

**IDU LED Brightness Control:** Lets the user adjust the brightness of the indoor unit's LED display.

**Inner Grooved Pipe:** Heat-exchanger piping with an internally grooved surface that creates oscillatory refrigerant motion for better heat dissipation.

**Intelligent Eye (Energy Saver and Auto ON/OFF):** A motion sensor that detects room occupancy and automatically adjusts operation (or switches the unit on/off) to save energy when no one is present.

## Feature glossary — efficiency, control, and durability

**Mobile App Control:** Lets the user control the AC from any location via a mobile app (requires Wi-Fi enabled model).

**Multi Layer Fire Safety:** Multiple built-in safety layers designed to protect against fire hazards.

**Patented DNNS Coating:** A patented anti-corrosive coating applied to enhance durability of AC components.

**Patented Streamer Discharge:** Discharges high-speed electrons to decompose harmful substances and germs, helping deliver germ-free, purer air.

**Patented Swing Compressor:** Daikin's proprietary compressor design used across its inverter split AC range for efficient, reliable operation.

**Power Airflow Dual Flaps:** Dual flap design that distributes airflow evenly to all corners of the room.

**Power Chill:** A boost mode that delivers immediate relief from hot temperatures by rapidly increasing cooling output.

**Power-Airflow On/Off Dual Flaps:** Dual flaps that provide even airflow distribution across all corners of the room (Non-Inverter Standard Series).

**Prefilter With Anti Bacterial Coating:** A washable pre-filter coated to inhibit bacterial growth, supporting air purification.

**Remote Backlit:** The remote control has a backlit display for easy use in low light.

**Self Diagnosis:** The unit can detect and indicate operational faults for hassle-free troubleshooting and servicing.

**Smart phone app control (Wi-Fi):** Lets the user control the AC remotely from a smartphone app from anywhere, via built-in Wi-Fi.

**Smell Proof Operation:** Design/operation feature intended to remove unpleasant odours for fresher air.

**Stabilizer Inside:** A built-in voltage stabilizer that allows the AC to operate smoothly through voltage fluctuations without needing an external stabilizer.

**Triple Display:** An IDU display that shows multiple pieces of information (mode, temperature, timer, etc.) for easy monitoring and control.

**Voice Control (Amazon Alexa and Google Home):** Enables voice control of the AC through Amazon Alexa or Google Home smart speakers.

## Special filters glossary

**3 Layer Filter:** Triple filtration process (pre-filter plus two additional layers) for enhanced dust and particle capture.

**Active Carbon Filter:** Activated carbon media that helps remove VOCs (volatile organic compounds) and odours from the air.

**Apataito Filter:** Filter media that helps remove airborne particles and odours.

**CO2 Filter and Cetachin:** Filter combination that traps odours through adsorption.

**PM1.0:** High-efficiency particulate filter that captures ultra-fine particles (PM1.0) to help improve indoor air quality.

**PM2.5 Filter:** Particulate filter that captures fine dust and particles (PM2.5) to help improve indoor air quality.

**Vitamin C Filter:** Filter infused with Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid) that helps remove certain harmful chemicals and toxins from the air.
